# Eval v3 (round four): router + rewritten source check (2026-09-20)

`--mode auto` over the same 56 questions. Grades: `eval/grades_v3_head.json`,
`eval/grades_v3_tail.json`. Drafts were byte-identical to round three, so head differences
come from the source check and from routing.

| | Head (32) | Long-tail (24) | All 56 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round three, always answer-first + check | 7.55 | 6.58 | 7.13 |
| Round three, always retrieval-first | 6.61 | 7.00 | 6.78 |
| Round four, router + conservative check | 7.30 | 6.54 | 6.97 |

Round four did not improve on round three; the differences are near grading noise (about 0.2)
but every component change measured neutral or negative:

- Conservative source check: helped 3 / neutral 18 / hurt 5 on the 26 checked head questions
  (round three on the same 26: 8 / 14 / 4). It removed padding and invented additions, but also
  nearly all useful additions, pinned "Correction" on correct claims (cmp-05, trv-02, med-02),
  and said "Sources agree" over errors the sources could have caught (num-03, syn-04, hop-02).
  An earlier variant with a promoted "Corrected answer:" headline was worse still (smoke test:
  it invented corrections and extrapolated a wrong boiling point) and was dropped.
- Router (first planned title resolved and under 5,000 monthly views -> retrieval-first):
  routed 19/24 long-tail and 6/32 head questions retrieval-first. Those 6 head questions
  scored 6.75 vs 7.67 for their round-three verified answers. The 19 long-tail ones scored
  7.16; the 5 that leaked to answer-first scored 4.2 (fabricated drafts, only partly repaired,
  one made worse by a name taken from the planner's wrong title). Five alternative routing
  rules simulated on round-three grades all scored lower than the current one.
- Six passages of 650 characters: fixed the silent-drop failures (tail-02 and tail-24 went
  to 8) but trimming article leads cost facts in six long-tail answers.

## Frozen pipeline (no further prompt iteration before the app exists)

- Router as is.
- Source check: round-three wording plus a 120-word cap, "not mentioned is not wrong", and
  source-subject binding.
- Article leads at full length, secondary sections trimmed to 650 characters, six passages,
  about 1,000 tokens of context.

The limiting factor for checking is the model (3B active, 2-bit), not the prompt. Remaining
known failures: planner never asks for the right article (tail-03), near-miss titles resolving
to the wrong article (tail-11 "Bagnes"), two-hop questions where the needed fact is not in the
retrieved passages (hop-04).
