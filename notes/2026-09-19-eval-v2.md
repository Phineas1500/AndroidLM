errors when the evidence was in hand (hop-04, num-01, num-03) and introduced errors by binding
facts to the wrong entity (cmp-04, exp-04). Redirects fixed the earlier title failures
(syn-02 4 -> 7, cmp-01 2.5 -> 6, med-03 5 -> 6); retrieval good/partial/miss 15/16/1.
Still unresolved: Sultanahmet, Neptune -> NEPTUNE, "Political system of ...", "Patagonian Andes".

VM timings (median): draft 63 s, source check pass 99 s (reads ~1270 tokens, writes ~160),
search 2.75 s.

## Router simulation (from existing grades, `scripts/router_views.py`)

Route to retrieval-first when the first planned article has fewer than N monthly views,
otherwise answer-first + check. N = 5000 sends 22/24 long-tail and 7/32 head questions to
retrieval-first and scores 7.19 overall (7.13 for always-verify, 7.65 for a perfect router).
The score gain is small because the source check already rescues most long-tail answers; the
real benefits are not showing a fabricated draft first and skipping the draft pass (~63 s).

## Bugs and fixes found this round

- `build_context` dropped passages beyond the character budget but they were still listed as
  sources (tail-02, probably tail-24). Fixed: only passages that reach the model are reported;
  long passages are cut at a sentence end; the subject article's best section goes in before
  other titles' leads.
- Six source checks hit the 300-token limit; raised to 450.
