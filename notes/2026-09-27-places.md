# Places: where to eat, drink and stay (2026-09-27)

Vitalik tried the bounty's apps, AndroidLM among them, and wrote that they were weakest at
specialized travel questions; his own test is "Tell me the best vegan restaurants in [city I am
currently in]", and none of the apps did it well. Ours could not: Wikipedia does not list
restaurants, and English Wikivoyage mentions vegan food in 1,134 of its 34,004 guides (3 times in
Lisbon's, 9 in Berlin's). Asked on the phone before this change, the app took 2 min 39 s over
"Tell me the best vegan restaurants in Lisbon" and recommended "Bloom", "Vegana", "The Green
Spot" and "Buddha Bar", described in detail and invented; the source check found nothing to
correct because the sources said nothing about restaurants.

## The data: places.db

One SQLite file, 1.7GB, built by `scripts/build_places.py` in about 6 minutes on a laptop from
local files (the downloads are about 11GB for Overture, 100MB for OpenStreetMap, 11MB for
GeoNames):

| Source | What | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Overture Maps places, release 2026-09-23.1 | categories under food_and_drink and lodging, not marked permanently closed, existence confidence 0.3 or more | 12,511,358 |
| OpenStreetMap (one query to QLever's copy of the planet, `scripts/fetch_osm_diet.py`, 68 s) | places with a `diet:*` tag or a vegan/vegetarian `cuisine` | 156,514 objects, 142,500 food places |
| | ... matched to an Overture place (similar name within 120 m), adding diet tags, opening hours and cuisine | 93,211 |
| | ... added as places of their own | 49,289 |
| GeoNames cities1000 | cities of 1,000 people or more, with Latin-script alternate names for those of 15,000 or more | 171,075 cities, 336,746 names |
| Wikivoyage (voyage.db) | Eat, Drink and Sleep listings in guides whose city resolves, matched by name near it | 164,859 listings, 83,732 matched |

The public Overpass servers timed out on the OpenStreetMap part (HTTP 504 even for a tiny
query); QLever answered the whole worldwide query in 68 s. Wikivoyage listings have no
coordinates in voyage.db, so a listing is matched to a place with the same name (or a close one
at the same house number) within the guide city's radius, food listings to food places and
Sleep listings to lodging; a random 25 of the Buenos Aires matches were all right.

Singapore 463, London 1,416, Mexico City 240, Cape Town 52, Istanbul 116, Seoul 98, Austin 113.

## The route

`scripts/places.py` (and `Places.kt`, which the golden test holds to it):

1. **Parse** the question: the kind of place (to eat, cafes, drinks, sweets, to stay), a diet
   (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal, kosher), one kind within it (a cuisine from the
   category names, or hostel, pub, coffee...), a price hint (cheap, luxury) and "near me". A
   question about food in general ("what is the food like in Lisbon", "traditional dishes") or
   about history stays on the Wikipedia route.
2. **Locate**: the words after "in/near/around" (or a capitalised run) against the city names,
   with a country or state after a comma ("Paris, Texas", "Cambridge, MA"). A city whose own
   name it is outranks one that only has it as an alternate name ("Porto" is also a name of
   Bordeaux); a state or province counts by its people ("Bali", "Tuscany"); national capitals
   count five times ("Washington"). "Near me" takes the phone's GPS position (no network) and
   widens from 2 to 5 to 10 km until five places match.
3. **Find** the places within the city's radius (3-25 km by population) on a 0.05-degree grid
   index, then rank: diet first (all about the diet; vegetarian for a vegan question; serves it;
   a few options), restaurants before cafes and shops when the question says "restaurants", then
   a score: listed in the travel guide, both sources agree, existence confidence, a website, not
   a chain, near the centre. A branch tagged vegan-only whose same-name branches say "vegan
   options" is overruled (one Honest Burgers in London). Without a diet only the best 400 by the
   question-free part of the score are read: "best restaurants in Tokyo" read 113,190 rows and
   took 6.6 s on the phone, now 0.24 s.
4. Nothing of the kind in the city: the radius doubles, twice, up to 50 km; still nothing, and
   the app says so without the model, instead of leaving the question to the Wikipedia route.
5. **Answer**: the list (12 places, with street, distance, opening hours, phone, website, the
   travel guide's words and a link to a map app) is on screen at once; the model reads the first
   six and recommends three to five in a line each, from the list only.

## On the phone (Pixel 8 Pro)

The list is on screen 0.1-0.35 s after the question. The first version of the prompt let the
model embellish ("near the riverfront", "traditional Portuguese vegan interpretations") and
then copy whole lines until it ran out of tokens (100 s); the prompt now asks for short lines
in its own words, only from the list, and the model reads six places instead of ten, with the
guide's words cut to 180 characters: 576-680 prompt tokens, answers of 150-220 tokens, done in
about 65-80 s.
