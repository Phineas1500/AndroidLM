# The Vitalik-style questions again, on 1.2.1 (2026-10-01)

The 61 questions of [2026-09-28-vitalik-bar.md](2026-09-28-vitalik-bar.md) were asked again on
the Pixel 8 Pro with release 1.2.1, which has:
- the Ethereum and cryptography library;
- the fuller restaurant answers;
- the engine's prefix cache.

The method on the phone was the same:
- every question from a cold app start;
- the three location questions with a test GPS position;
- each question started at 30.5 C or four minutes after the previous one.

The answers are in `eval/answers_phone_vitalik121.jsonl`. They were graded twice, and the two
gradings disagree on restaurants. Both are reported here.

## 1. Graded side by side with the 9/28 answers: 53% -> 65%

For each question one grader saw three answers in a random order: the 9/28 phone answer, the
1.2.1 phone answer and the web reference. There was one grader per group, and it was not told
which answer was which (`scripts/answer_sets.py`; `eval/sets_vitalik121_*.json`, keys and grades
alongside).

| Group | 9/28 app | 1.2.1 app | 1.2.1 against 9/28, per question | Errors 9/28 / 1.2.1 / reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurants (20) | 46% | **72%** | better on 19, worse on 0 | 9 / 16 / 1 |
| Crypto (20) | 44% | **54%** | better on 10, worse on 5 | 30 / 8 / 0 |
| Travel, emergencies, arithmetic (21) | 68% | 69% | better on 9, worse on 7 | 27 / 29 / 1 |
| **All 61** | **53%** | **65%** | | 66 / 53 / 2 |

- **The reference** was the best answer on all 61 questions.
- **The web search quota had run out** for all three graders. They checked facts by fetching
  pages directly (OpenStreetMap, HappyCow, the restaurants' own sites, eips.ethereum.org, NIST,
  gov.uk) and from their own knowledge.
- **Closures:** the restaurant grader looked up about 60 places this way. It found few closed ones
  (see section 2).

## 2. Graded in pairs against the reference, as on 9/28: 64% -> 62%

As on 9/28, each 1.2.1 answer was graded against the reference alone, in blind pairs, one grader
per group (`eval/pairs_vitalik121.json`, key and grades alongside):

| Group | 9/28 | 1.2.1 | App errors 9/28 / 1.2.1 | Reference errors 9/28 / 1.2.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurants | 68% | 57% | 21 / 86 | 6 / 27 |
| Crypto | 52% | 56% | 32 / 6 | 0 / 0 |
| Travel | 67% | 67% | 14 / 8 | 2 / 1 |
| Emergencies | 74% | 73% | 2 / 4 | 1 / 0 |
| Arithmetic | 82% | 81% | 1 / 2 | 0 / 0 |
| **All 61** | **64%** | **62%** | 70 / 106 | 9 / 28 |

**Restaurants are the whole difference.** This grader still had web search for part of its
checks. It looked for closures across the answer and the list of places the app shows with it.
- **On the app:** it found 44 closed places, half of its 86 errors.
- **On the reference:** it was much harsher too: 27 errors, against 6 from the 9/28 grader.

## What to make of it

- **Before and after belong in one sitting.** Two graders gave the same 9/28 restaurant answers
  68% and 46% of the reference. Absolute shares from different graders differ by 10 to 20 points,
  so the before-and-after comparison is section 1. It agrees with the side-by-side rounds of
  [2026-09-29-places-answers.md](2026-09-29-places-answers.md) (45% -> 71%) and of the pack
  ([2026-09-28-ethereum-pack.md](2026-09-28-ethereum-pack.md), crypto 47% -> 58%).
- **Closed places are the restaurant answers' main error.** A grader that checks every place finds
  many more of them, and an answer that names more places names more closed ones. The data cannot
  tell them apart (Overture's `update_time` is the dataset's refresh date; confidence and
  OpenStreetMap presence do not separate open from closed). It is the next problem for restaurants.
- **The other groups did not move,** as expected: 1.2 changed nothing about them.
- **The prefix cache changes the wording of answers slightly.** These answers were written with it
  on, and travel, emergencies and arithmetic scored the same as before (68% and 69% side by side).

  timings are slow.
